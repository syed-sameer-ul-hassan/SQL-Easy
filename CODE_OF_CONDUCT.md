# SQL Easy - Code of Conduct

> Version 1.1.0 | Effective: 2026-05-25

---

## Our Pledge

SQL Easy is a security research tool used by professionals, students, and researchers. We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to ensure inclusion regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Our Standards

### Behavior that builds a healthy community

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Keeping security research ethical and within scope of authorized targets

### Behavior that will not be tolerated

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Using this tool maliciously against systems you do not own or have no written authorization to test
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional security setting

---

## Ethical Use Requirement

SQL Easy is a penetration testing tool. Contributors and users of this project are expected to:

- Only use the tool on systems they own or have **explicit written authorization** to test
- Comply with all applicable local, national, and international laws
- Responsibly disclose any vulnerabilities found during research
- Never use this tool to harm individuals, organizations, or critical infrastructure

Violating ethical use norms is grounds for immediate removal from the project community.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by:

- Opening a private GitHub Security Advisory at the repository
- Using the bug/contact portal at [bug.orildo.sbs](https://bug.orildo.sbs)

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly. The project team is committed to maintaining confidentiality of reporters.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct may be removed from the project team.
